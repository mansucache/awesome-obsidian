# From a question to a sourced answer

## Goal

Compare sources and connect each conclusion to its evidence.

## Steps

- Define a question narrow enough to answer, such as how a team records decisions during remote work. Write what would count as a useful answer.
- Create a source note for each paper or article. Record its author, date, link and relevant page or section; keep direct quotations distinct from your interpretation.
- Extract claims into a comparison note. For each claim, link the supporting source and record its context, method and limits.
- Look for disagreement. Check whether sources describe different populations, time periods or definitions before treating their conclusions as contradictory.
- Draft an answer with a source beside each factual claim. Mark unanswered questions and identify the next source needed.

## Acceptance

- Each conclusion leads to a source and a specific supporting passage.
- The answer distinguishes evidence, interpretation and open questions.
