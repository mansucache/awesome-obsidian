# From a saved article to a usable note

## Why it is here
Saving an article and being able to use it later are different outcomes. This workflow joins source capture, interpretation and retrieval without requiring a plugin stack.

## Use cases
Read one public article related to a question you already have. Use your vault with ordinary notes and internal links. No account, paid service or AI is required for the baseline.

## Steps
- Create a question note, for example: What makes a reading note useful a month later?
- Create a source note with the article title, URL, author if known and the date you read it. Manually copy only the excerpt you need and distinguish it from your own writing.
- Add three short parts: the claim you understood, the evidence you found, and a limitation or unresolved question. Follow the source link to check your interpretation.
- Link the source note from the question note. Write a sentence explaining why it belongs there; do not rely on the link alone.
- Close the source note. Return through the question note and locate both the source and your explanation. If you cannot, improve the title or link context before adding tools.

## Acceptance
- The original source can be reopened from the note.
- Quoted material and your own interpretation are distinguishable.
- The question note leads to a relevant explanation without searching the entire vault.
- A later review has a concrete unresolved question to work on.

## Optional extensions
Use [Web Clipper](web-clipper.md) when capture becomes repetitive. Add [Templater](templater.md) for repeated note creation, then [Dataview](dataview.md) only when a manual reading index becomes burdensome. [Sync](obsidian-sync.md) serves cross-device access; [Obsidian Skills](obsidian-skills.md) can be evaluated separately on a copy.

## Limits
A clipped page can lose formatting or disappear upstream. A polished AI summary does not replace checking the evidence.
