# From course notes to usable knowledge

## Goal

Practice recall, record mistakes and apply concepts in a small task.

## Steps

- Choose one learning outcome that can be demonstrated, such as explaining a concept or solving a class of problems. Link the course materials in a topic note.
- After a lesson, close the material and explain the key idea from memory. Reopen the source to correct gaps and label uncertain points.
- Turn difficult points into short questions with checkable answers. Ask one thing per question and attach the source explanation.
- Schedule a review and answer before revealing your notes. Record whether each mistake came from missing facts, confused concepts or failed application.
- Apply the concept in a small new task. Add the result and any remaining difficulty to the topic note, then adjust the next review.

## Acceptance

- You can explain the concept without copying the source.
- A new example or task demonstrates application, and mistakes have explanations.

## Optional extensions

Use [Spaced Repetition](spaced-repetition.md) when you want scheduled review; keep questions focused using [atomic notes](atomic-notes.md).
