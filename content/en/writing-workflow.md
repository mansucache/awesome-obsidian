# From linked notes to a finished draft

## Goal

Turn source notes into an outline, draft and exportable article.

## Steps

- Create a project note with a reader, a question and the main point the article should establish. Define the intended length and delivery format.
- Collect links to useful notes under a few provisional headings. Add one sentence explaining what each note contributes; leave unrelated material outside the outline.
- Order the headings so each section answers a question raised by the previous one. Identify missing evidence before expanding the draft.
- Draft one section at a time in your own words. Keep source links beside factual passages so revisions do not detach claims from evidence.
- Read the draft from the beginning without opening background notes. Remove repetition, verify quotations and export a copy in the required format.

## Acceptance

- The draft answers its stated question and every section advances that answer.
- Links, quotations and the exported document have been checked.
